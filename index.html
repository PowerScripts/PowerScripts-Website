Power Your Terminal

An open-source collection of interactive PowerShell scripts, CLI games with 8-bit sound effects, and safe visual pranks.

🚀 Welcome to PowerScripts

PowerScripts lets you execute rich terminal experiences directly inside your PowerShell console without downloading bulky installers. Run games, utilities, and fun simulations with a single command.

🎮 What You'll Find

  • Full CLI Games: Smooth ASCII/ANSI animations and dynamic rendering.
  • Native 8-bit Audio: Soundtracks and sound effects synthesized directly in your console.
  • Safe Visual Pranks: Hollywood-style hacking screen simulations to prank your friends safely.

Ready to try? Head over to the Scripts section to copy and run scripts instantly.
